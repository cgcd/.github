The **Centre gouvernemental de cyberdéfense** (Government Cyber Defence Centre) protects the digital infrastructure of Québec's public administration. Part of the [Ministère de la Cybersécurité et du Numérique](https://www.quebec.ca/gouvernement/ministere/cybersecurite-numerique), it monitors threats to government departments and public bodies, handles vulnerabilities detected across government, and coordinates the [Réseau gouvernemental de cyberdéfense](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/presentation/ecosysteme/reseau-gouvernemental-cyberdefense) (government cyber defence network).

This GitHub organization is home to its development team. Our repositories are private: they help defend government systems.

The website is mostly in French; the vulnerability reporting pages are also available in English.

Reports are governed by the [conditions of use](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/en/report/conditions-of-use), and contributors are acknowledged on the [contributors page](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/en/report/contributors). For tooling: [`security.txt`](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/.well-known/security.txt). To write to CERT-QC encrypted, see our [security policy](https://github.com/cgcd/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Careers

The CGCD is looking for talent to work on high-impact mandates in a dynamic, committed team. Profiles include [cyber defence solutions developer](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/carrieres/developpeuse-developpeur-solutions-cyberdefense), as well as cybersecurity, penetration testing and incident management roles.

[See the profiles and job offers (in French) →](https://www.cyber.gouv.qc.ca/carrieres)
